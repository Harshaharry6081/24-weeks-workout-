Approach: Prioritise consistent protein from whole foods (eggs, chicken, fish, lentils, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese). Eat enough to support training and recovery. A moderate, sustainable deficit or maintenance is the goal — not aggressive restriction. Record dietary adjustments and note their effect on energy and body weight over time. Progressive Overload TrackerRecord progressions by exercise · Technique must be confirmed before advancing

Progression rule: First increase clean repetitions to the top of the prescribed range across all sets. Then increase backpack load or advance to a harder variation. Do NOT progress if you experience: joint pain · technique breakdown · excessive soreness · inadequate sleep or recovery. Technique Confirmed = Yes is required before logging a new load or variation.
