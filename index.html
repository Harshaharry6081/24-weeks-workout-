Programme OverviewWhat happens across 24 weeks

Weeks 1–8

Foundation

Master technique on every exercise. Build consistency. Focus on body weight movements and light backpack loads. No ego lifting.

Weeks 9–16

Development

Gradually increase load and repetitions. Introduce harder variations when prescribed reps are consistently hit with clean form.

Weeks 17–24

Refinement

Higher loads, advanced variations, consolidate habits. Final measurements, photos and summary at Week 24.
